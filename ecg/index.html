Load an ECG → filter → Pan-Tompkins QRS → P/QRS/T delineation → intervals → rule-based draft read.

⚠ Automated draft – not a diagnosis. Output is generated by simple rules and may be wrong (noise, lead choice, unusual morphology). Every finding must be confirmed by a clinician reviewing the original tracing and the patient. Not a medical device; no STEMI detection.

1 · Load ECG

CSV/TXT: one column per lead, optional header and time column. WFDB: select the .hea and .dat files together (formats 16, 212, 80, 61).
EXPERIMENTAL. Digitizes a single-lead rhythm strip photo/scan by dark-pixel tracing over a red/pink grid. Works on clean, flat, straight images only. Amplitudes and intervals are approximate – rhythm/rate only.
The image opens in the large “Photo digitizer” panel for point picking.
Import options
Auto units: peak-to-peak <20 → mV, <20000 → µV, offset integers → ADC. Sampling rate comes from the time column, JSON fs or WFDB header; otherwise 250 Hz is assumed (set it here).

    Beat measurements

    Methods: zero-phase Butterworth/biquad filters (baseline, low-pass, 50/60 Hz notch); Pan-Tompkins QRS detection (5–15 Hz band-pass, derivative, squaring, 150 ms integration, adaptive thresholds, search-back, T-wave discrimination); slope-envelope QRS boundaries, windowed P detection with 10%-amplitude onset, tangent-method T end. QTc Bazett = QT/√RR, Fridericia = QT/∛RR. Rules are deliberately simple; see README for limitations.
    Demo data: synthetic (generated) and short excerpts from the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database (Moody & Mark 2001; Goldberger et al., PhysioNet, Circulation 2000), Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0.
    Everything runs locally in your browser; no data leaves the device.
